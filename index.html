Carter Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 8, 2026

Overview

Carter ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Carter mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Vehicle information (make, model, year, mileage)
  • Maintenance records and service history
  • Photos of receipts (stored locally on your device)
  • Cost information for services

Automatically Collected Information

  • App usage statistics
  • Device information (iOS version, device type)
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide maintenance tracking functionality
  • To send push notifications for upcoming services
  • To improve app performance and user experience
  • To provide customer support

Data Storage

  • All vehicle and maintenance data is stored locally on your device
  • We do not store your personal vehicle information on our servers
  • Photos and receipts remain on your device only

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties. Your data stays on your device.

Push Notifications

Carter uses push notifications to remind you of upcoming maintenance. You can disable these in your device settings at any time.

Children's Privacy

Carter is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your Rights

  • Delete all app data by uninstalling Carter
  • Disable push notifications in iOS Settings
  • Export your maintenance records as PDF

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app.
